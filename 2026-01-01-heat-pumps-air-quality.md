Replacing gas boilers with heat pumps removes a local source of NO<sub>x</sub>, but uptake is unlikely to be even across the country. This study models the spatiotemporal adoption of heat pumps in England and Wales to 2050 and quantifies how the resulting emission changes affect air quality disparities between communities.
