Hydrogen combustion engines produce no CO<sub>2</sub> at the tailpipe, but burning hydrogen in air can still form nitrogen oxides (NO<sub>x</sub>). Working with J.C. Bamford Excavators Ltd (JCB), this study evaluated NO<sub>x</sub> emissions from a hydrogen internal combustion engine designed for off-road and construction machinery.

Initial analysis was submitted as evidence to the UK Government consultation *Enabling road use of hydrogen-powered non-road mobile machinery*.
