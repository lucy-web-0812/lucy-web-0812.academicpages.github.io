I'm a final-year PhD candidate at the [Wolfson Atmospheric Chemistry Laboratories](https://www.york.ac.uk/chemistry/research/wacl/), University of York (thesis submission expected January 2027). My research applies large-scale statistical analysis and spatio-temporal modelling to **air pollution, decarbonisation policy and inequalities**.

My thesis, *Air Quality Implications from Net Zero Policies and Pathways in the UK*, asks a simple question: as the UK decarbonises, who breathes cleaner air, and who gets left behind?

Research highlights
======
* **Hydrogen engines for construction machinery.** Working with J.C. Bamford Excavators Ltd (JCB), I evaluated NO<sub>x</sub> emissions from a hydrogen internal combustion engine for non-road machinery. Early analysis was submitted to a UK Government consultation and grew into a [peer-reviewed paper](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4EM00448E).
* **Is NO<sub>x</sub> regulation consistent?** An economy-wide review of NO<sub>x</sub> emission limits across UK combustion sectors, putting limits written in different units onto one comparable scale. [Published](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4EA00149D) and covered by *The Guardian*.
* **Heat pumps and air quality inequality.** Modelling the spatiotemporal uptake of heat pumps across England and Wales to 2050 and what it means for disparities in NO<sub>x</sub> emissions (in review).

Policy and open tools
======
I enjoy building open, evidence-based tools with government and industry partners. During a six-month placement with Defra's Air Quality and Industrial Emissions Team I worked on data-driven tools supporting air quality policy, and I've built public dashboards such as the National Atmospheric Emissions Inventory Visualiser and an Inequalities Modeller. See [Open Tools](/portfolio/).

Before my PhD
======
I was a Higher Statistical Officer in Quality Assurance at HMRC, and I'm a badged Government Statistical Service statistician and member of the Royal Statistical Society. I graduated from the University of Cambridge with a First Class MSci in Natural Sciences (Earth Sciences), where my research project with the British Antarctic Survey reconstructed a palaeoclimate record from sub-Antarctic ice cores.
