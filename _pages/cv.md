Profile
======
Final-year PhD candidate (thesis submission expected January 2027) applying large-scale statistical analysis and spatio-temporal modelling to air pollution, decarbonisation policy and inequalities. Two first-author papers, with a third in review. Experienced in developing open, evidence-based tools with government and industry partners, including a placement in the UK Government's Air Quality and Industrial Emissions Team.

Education
======
* **PhD in Atmospheric Chemistry**, Wolfson Atmospheric Chemistry Laboratories, University of York, Oct 2023 – Jan 2027 (expected submission)
  * Thesis: *Air Quality Implications from Net Zero Policies and Pathways in the UK*
  * Evaluated NO<sub>x</sub> emissions from a hydrogen internal combustion engine for non-road machinery in collaboration with J.C. Bamford Excavators Ltd (JCB); initial analysis was submitted to a UK Government consultation and expanded into a peer-reviewed paper.
  * Produced an economy-wide review of NO<sub>x</sub> emission regulation across UK combustion sectors, converting limits in differing units onto a consistent scale; published and covered by *The Guardian*.
  * Modelling the spatiotemporal uptake of heat pumps in England and Wales to quantify changes in NO<sub>x</sub> emissions inequality to 2050.
* **MSci Natural Sciences (Earth Sciences)**, Murray Edwards College, University of Cambridge, Oct 2018 – Jul 2022
  * First Class Honours
  * Awarded the Rosemary Murray Scholarship prize for academic achievement.
  * Independent research project with the British Antarctic Survey on two sub-Antarctic ice cores, extracting a palaeoclimatic record from chemical, isotopic and physical data.
  * Final-year courses included Atmospheric Chemistry, the Earth System & Climate Change, Natural Hazards and Frontiers of Ice Core Science.

Professional experience
======
* **PhD Placement, Air Quality and Industrial Emissions Team** (6 months, during PhD)
  * Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Defra)
  * Contributed to data-driven tools and insights supporting evidence-based air quality policy, including work linking health data to emissions.
  * Started a 'Coffee and Coding' club to upskill the team in data analysis using R and Python.
* **Graduate Teaching Assistant**, Oct 2024 – Jul 2026
  * Department of Chemistry, University of York
  * First-year Mathematics for Chemists: delivered workshops to >100 students, adapted teaching to individual needs and checked exam marking.
  * Awarded the Roger J Mawby Demonstrating Prize for outstanding contribution to teaching and learning at York.
* **Higher Statistical Officer, Quality Assurance**, Nov 2022 – Jul 2023
  * His Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC)
  * Ensured the statistical robustness of departmental outputs through in-depth model reviews; built learning resources for a curated R package and hosted knowledge-sharing sessions.
  * Badged Government Statistical Service statistician; member of the Royal Statistical Society.
